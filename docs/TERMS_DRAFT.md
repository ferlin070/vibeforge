# Terms of service — draft for operator review

**Not effective or approved.** This is a proposed operational outline, not binding terms or legal advice.

## Service scope

VibeForge provides software planning, editable specifications, AI-assisted candidate generation, manual agent handoff and advisory quality/release tools. It does not execute coding agents, independently verify implementation reports, guarantee correct AI output or deploy the user's projects. Users review generated content and retain responsibility for their software and release decisions.

## Accounts and credentials

Users should provide accurate account information, keep credentials secure, use only authorized repositories and API keys, and respect provider usage limits. Third-party AI providers may charge the user's account. VibeForge must not be used to bypass authorization, access private networks through gateways, expose others' confidential data, abuse infrastructure or violate applicable law.

## Content and backups

Users should have permission to submit project content and review any AI-generated or third-party material before reuse. Local projects remain in the browser; optional cloud snapshots are explicit backups, not automatic synchronization. Users should export important work. Review the proposed privacy policy for data handling. No guarantee of uninterrupted availability, recovery or suitability is offered in this draft.

## Required operator decisions

Before publication, confirm the contracting legal entity, eligibility and age rules, acceptable use enforcement, intellectual property terms and licenses, pricing and refunds if applicable, service availability, warranties and liability language permitted by applicable law, termination, dispute resolution, governing law, contact details, effective date and change notification. Obtain qualified legal review and explicit operator approval. No payment plan, SLA or open-source license is established by this draft.
